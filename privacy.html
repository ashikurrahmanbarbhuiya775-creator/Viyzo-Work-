Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page explains, in general terms, how Viyzo Worker may process information when you use the app. It is a draft policy for review and must be checked against the final app, service providers, and applicable law before public release.

Important: Do not submit Aadhaar/PAN scans, bank passwords, card PINs, OTPs, or other sensitive documents through ordinary chat or free-text fields. Only use an explicitly provided, secure verification flow when it is available.

Information that may be processed

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers used to sign in.
  • Profile information: display name, country, preferred language, and information you choose to provide.
  • Work activity: job listings, applications, task submissions, review status, earnings records, and payout status where those features are enabled.
  • Support and diagnostics: information you send when requesting help and technical details needed to investigate an error.
  • Payment and verification information: only when the relevant feature and provider are configured. Do not assume payments, identity verification, or payouts are live unless the app explicitly confirms the provider flow.

How information may be used

Information may be used to authenticate accounts, display available work, process applications and submissions, maintain work and earnings records, prevent fraud, support users, protect service security, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

Viyzo Worker uses Supabase for backend services such as authentication and database functions. If payment, hosting, analytics, or identity-verification providers are enabled, relevant information may be shared with those providers only as needed for the feature and subject to the applicable provider terms. This draft does not confirm that any payment or payout provider is live.

Storage and security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect account and work data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Access controls, retention periods, backup practices, and incident-response procedures must be reviewed before launch.

Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate security needs. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the email below. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Children

Viyzo Worker is not intended for children under 18. Do not create an account if you are under 18.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app and its service providers change. The date at the top will be updated when this page is revised.
