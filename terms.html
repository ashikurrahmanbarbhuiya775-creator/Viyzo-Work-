1. About Viyzo

Viyzo Worker is being developed as a platform to help businesses organise task-based work and help workers discover and manage opportunities. Access to features may vary during testing and rollout.

2. Eligibility and accounts

The service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. You must provide accurate account information, keep your sign-in credentials confidential, and promptly report suspected unauthorised access. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account, subject to applicable law.

3. Company responsibilities

Companies must provide accurate business and job information, describe the scope, requirements, deadlines, acceptance criteria, and compensation clearly, and only post work they are authorised to offer. A company must not ask a worker to begin work on the assumption that an unconfirmed payment has been received.

4. Worker responsibilities

Workers should review the task description, eligibility, deadline, review criteria, and payment conditions before accepting work. Workers must submit their own genuine work and must not misuse another person's account or personal information.

5. Funding, fees, and payouts

Payment availability depends on provider approval, country coverage, account verification, and successful testing. A payment is not confirmed merely because a browser reports success. A job should only be released after the platform confirms the required funds. Worker earnings are not a bank payout; a transfer is complete only after the payout provider confirms it. Applicable platform fees, taxes, refunds, reversals, and payout timing must be shown before a live transaction is enabled.

6. Cancellations, disputes, and prohibited use

Do not use Viyzo for unlawful work, fraud, deceptive listings, harassment, discrimination, spam, or attempts to bypass payment and safety controls. Cancellation, refund, dispute, evidence, and appeal rules must be displayed for the relevant live market before work is accepted. Contact support if you see a suspected scam or payment mismatch.

7. Availability and changes

Features may be changed, paused, or withdrawn during development, maintenance, security response, or provider outages. We will aim to communicate material changes clearly. No job availability or income is guaranteed.

8. Privacy

Personal information is handled as described in the Privacy Policy, which must be read together with these terms. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card PINs, or banking credentials to support.
