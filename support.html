How can we help?

Contact the Viyzo Worker team for account access, company onboarding, worker experience, privacy requests, or to report a security concern.

Contact support

Send an email with a short description of the issue. Include the app version and approximate time if reporting a technical problem. Never include your password, OTP, card PIN, full bank credentials, or identity document scans in an ordinary email.

Email Viyzo Support

Support email: ashikurrahmanbarbhuiya775@gmail.com

Security warning: Viyzo support will not ask you to share your password, one-time password, card PIN, or banking login. If someone requests these details while claiming to represent Viyzo, do not share them.

What to include

  • Whether you are contacting as a company or worker.
  • A short description of what happened and the screen or step where it occurred.
  • App version, device model, and time of the issue, if relevant.
  • A non-sensitive reference number, if one is shown.

Privacy and account requests

For access, correction, or deletion requests, write “Privacy Request” in your email subject. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. See the Privacy Policy for the current draft.

Current service status

Viyzo is under development and testing. This page is a contact route, not a live ticketing or 24/7 support guarantee. Payment and payout availability must be confirmed inside the app before a user relies on it.
