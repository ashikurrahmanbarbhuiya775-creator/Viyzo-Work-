# Viyzo Brand Foundation

## Brand ambition
Build Viyzo into a trusted work platform that helps legitimate companies find capable people and helps workers discover clear, fairly described opportunities. Earn recognition through reliability and real outcomes—not exaggerated promises.

## Brand promise
**Work with trust. Grow without borders.**

This is a proposed brand line, not a claim of trademark registration or global availability.

## Brand principles
1. **Trust before hype** — never present demo data, unverified jobs, or estimated earnings as guaranteed.
2. **Clear opportunities** — show who posted a job, what the work is, requirements, deadline, funding/review status, and how payment works.
3. **Fairness and transparency** — clearly explain fees, worker share, company obligations, and payout status before anyone commits.
4. **Security and privacy** — collect only necessary information, protect accounts, and explain data use in plain language.
5. **Accessible worldwide** — design for small screens, readable contrast, simple language, and future localization.
6. **Prove every claim** — public marketing must match features that are actually live and tested.

## Visual direction
- **Core palette:** deep midnight navy #101014, vivid indigo/blue accents, white text, and restrained neutral greys.
- **Style:** confident, modern, uncluttered, human, and professional; avoid excessive emojis, fake counters, and income guarantees.
- **Typography:** use readable system sans-serif typography consistently.
- **Logo/icon:** create one distinctive, simple mark that remains recognizable at small launcher-icon size. Do not use the robot emoji as the permanent brand mark.
- **Consistency:** app, website, supervisor dashboard, help pages, and store assets should share the same name, colors, tone, and support contact.

## Voice and wording
- Say what the product does in plain language.
- Label unavailable features honestly (for example, “Payments not connected yet”).
- Never claim “guaranteed income”, “number one worldwide”, or “trusted by millions” without evidence.
- Explain next steps and errors calmly, with a clear action users can take.

## Release gates before public launch
- [ ] Verify the final launcher icon, app name, screenshots, and visual consistency.
- [ ] Test sign-up, email confirmation, login, logout, password reset, and session handling.
- [ ] Test company onboarding, job review, funding, worker application, task completion, and payout status end-to-end.
- [ ] Confirm payment-provider onboarding, webhook verification, refunds/disputes, and ledger reconciliation before accepting real money.
- [ ] Review database policies, authentication settings, secret handling, and security advisories.
- [ ] Check privacy policy against actual SDKs, permissions, and collected data.
- [ ] Publish clear support/contact and terms pages.
- [ ] Verify name, domain, social handles, and trademark availability before spending on registration or claiming exclusivity.
- [ ] Run accessibility, low-network, small-screen, and crash tests on real devices.
- [ ] Release gradually to a small test group, fix issues, then expand.
